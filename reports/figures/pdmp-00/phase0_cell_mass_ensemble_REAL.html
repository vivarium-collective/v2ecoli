Phase 0 cell mass ensemble (real, N=32)

real-dataN=32 × 600s × stride=5s

Real cell-mass(t) trajectories across N=32 master seeds, snapshot every 5 model-seconds. Spaghetti (faint blue) shows individual replicates; thick line is ensemble mean; bands are ±1 SD and ±2 SD. The ensemble grows smoothly from ~1265 fg at t=0 to ~1461 fg at t=600 s (one doubling cycle target is ~1× growth, ~ doubling-time minutes). The narrow SD band confirms low cross-seed divergence in mass, consistent with the constraint-bound nature of the FBA-driven biomass accumulation.
