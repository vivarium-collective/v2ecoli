◆ v-sweep-0.8e-3-band-stable computational novel literature
Sweeping the dnaA RNA-synthesis probability down to v = 0.8e-3 holds the DnaA monomer pool oscillating band-stable within [300,800] across the steady generations with a clean oriC cycle, at the cost of a sub-physiological mRNA initiation rate — establishing a genuine tension between the in-band pool and the ~1/min rate target at this initial condition.
in run dnaa1-v08e3-2026-06-08
◆ v-sweep-5point-choose-the-V computational novel literature
Across a 5-point sweep V ∈ {0.8,0.9,1.0,1.1,1.7}×1e-3, the band-stable DnaA pool (whole oscillation in [300,800], only at V=0.8e-3) and the ~1/min dnaA initiation rate (only at V=0.9–1.1e-3) occupy non-overlapping V regions, so no single value of the dnaA synthesis probability satisfies both acceptance layers at the dnaa-0 succinate initial condition — the in-band pool requires sacrificing the rate, and vice versa.
in run dnaa1-v08e3-2026-06-08
◆ v11-15gen-equilibration-overshoots-band computational novel literature
At the now-withdrawn candidate V=1.1e-3, a completed 15-generation run shows the DnaA monomer cycle-mean does NOT equilibrate inside [300,800] — it ramps over the ceiling (peak cycle-mean 976 at gen 9, within-cycle peaks ~1300, gens 6-12 at or above 800) and then relaxes back to 485 by gen 15, an overshoot-then-undershoot rather than an in-band plateau; the original 7-gen in-band reading was a pre-equilibration artifact, confirming that no single constitutive V satisfies both the band and the ~1/min rate. The cell cycle stays healthy throughout (every generation divides, oriC 1↔2, no reinitiation), and the FBA GLP_NOFEAS infeasibilities peak at gen 9 with the DnaA overshoot and vanish by gens 11-15.
in run dnaa1_v1p1e-3_long

✓ Resolved uncertainties

  • Mechanism A (runtime overwrite of TU00259[c]'s per-promoter init_prob) is the right lever for dnaA expression — sweeping V down to 0.8e-3 holds the DnaA monomer cycle-mean band-stable in [300,800] for steady gens 4-7 (337/338/360/415, peaks <800) with a clean oriC 1↔2 cycle. The chosen V undershoots the ~1/min init-rate target (0.46–0.71/min); the rate hits target at V≈1.0e-3, where the pool ramps out of band — the two acceptance layers are in tension.
  • monomer_counts[3861] (PD03831[c], idx 3861) already aggregates all DnaA forms, so it is usable directly as the DnaA pool observable (Rashmi build-note resolved).
  • The in-band DnaA pool is the downstream CONSEQUENCE of hitting the initiation-rate target, not an independent knob (the acceptance is reframed as a rate check).
  • {'The init-rate vs band-stable-pool tension': 'at the chosen V=0.8e-3 the DnaA pool is band-stable but the init rate (0.46–0.71/min) is sub-physiological; at V≈1.0e-3 the rate is on target but the pool ramps out of band by gen 7. No single V in this sweep satisfies BOTH layers at the dnaa-0 succinate initial condition — resolving this may need a different lever (e.g. cell-cycle-coupled synthesis or a different starting DnaA pool).'}
  • The lineage starts from dnaa-0's low (~12 monomer) baseline and RAMPS for several generations before settling; "steady state" is reached only by gen 4 even at the chosen V. Whether a longer lineage drifts further (the 0.8e-3 cycle-mean still creeps 337→415) is untested.
  • All sweep runs are single-seed (seed 1); cross-seed stability of the in-band pool is untested.
