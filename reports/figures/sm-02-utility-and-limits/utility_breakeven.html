What a cheap emulator buys you

The linear emulator swept 5,000 250-step trajectories in 0.02s — the same sweep on the WCM would take ~24 hours (~3,770,550× per trajectory). It pays back its 8 training sims after only ~8 evaluations. The expensive part is generating training data, not the emulator — so a cheap, honest emulator of a smooth observable is worth building even when a neural net isn't.
