Question & approach

Question. Is the v2ecoli wild-type baseline still "the same E. coli" as vEcoli (v1) when measured at LARGE ensemble scale? This study applies the PR #134 behavioral report-card approach (population_phenotype_basal in its v1↔v2 *equivalence* reference mode) to a large multi-generation baseline ensemble — 16 seeds × 16 generations — and grades v2's emergent population phenotype against a matched vEcoli 16×16 reference within tolerance. #134 exercised the same loop at 8×16 as a demo; this study scales it to a population large enough to tighten the omics/flux equivalence axes and to read v2's multi-generation behavior (the in-process sawtooth, #142) honestly against v1.

Key assumptions

  • A matched 16×16 vEcoli reference can be generated from the in-repo vEcoli source and pinned with pin_vecoli_equivalence_reference.py; v1 and v2 share cistron/monomer/flux ordering exactly, so the omics/flux vectors align positionally with no ID remapping (verified at 8×16 in #134).

Follow-ups & decisions

Discovery implications

Where this study's results leave the mechanism model — and what to investigate next.

✓ Resolved uncertainties

  • The #134 8×16 equivalence picture HOLDS at 16×16: Physiology / Composition / Ribosomes within tolerance (12 axes ✓, 6 minor drift), the overall exchange-flux fingerprint near-identical (R²=0.9989), the exchange-flux metabolic divergence (O₂ -40%, CO₂ -20%) persisting (issue #143), and gene expression highly correlated but below the strict 0.99 band (transcriptome R²=0.946, proteome R²=0.961 vs ~0.93–0.94 at 8×16). The larger sample tightened the statistics without changing the qualitative verdict.
  • A matched 16×16 vEcoli reference IS feasible from the in-repo sibling vEcoli (CovertLab/vEcoli @ b237873e) via its own Nextflow workflow. It ran to its natural maximum of 185 cells (9/16 single-daughter lineages completed all 16 gens; 7 truncated at deterministic FBA metabolic dead-ends — 256 unreachable for this seed set). 185 cells is a large, valid reference (> the 128-cell 8×16 demo).
  • v2's single-process meta-composite is impractically slow for 16×16 (~4 min/cell sequential → ~17 h); the new parallel-by-default multi-seed path (run_seeds_parallel) cut it to ~3.6 h for the full 256-cell ensemble. v2 completed all 16/16 lineages (256 cells); 24/256 generations hit the duration cap without dividing (the #142 sawtooth), surfaced by the card's sim-health banner.

● Remaining uncertainties

  • The gene-expression 'mismatch' is a strict-band artifact (R²=0.946 vs a 0.99 threshold inherited from the self-pin template). The principled cross-impl form — TOST + per-axis δ equivalence margins — would convert this into an explicit equivalence-margin verdict rather than a hard fail. Nice-to-have, not blocking.
  • Root cause of the O₂/CO₂ respiratory-exchange divergence (issue #143) is not resolved here — this study confirms it is stable and localized at scale, it does not explain it.
