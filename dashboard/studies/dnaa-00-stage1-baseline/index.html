Acetate is used here for vEcoli equivalence only — the actual Stage 1 baseline (glycerol) doesn't have a side-by-side vEcoli implementation to compare against.

**[Infrastructure fix]** D-period division trigger is correctly wired in v2ecoli. The fast `with_aa` condition's observed cycle dropped 45 → 22.5 min after MarkDPeriod.divide was wired in, matching vEcoli within rounding. (`with_aa` is a third condition used as an unrelated sanity baseline — neither the deliverable nor the diagnostic; it's the strongest signal for "is the D-period trigger firing on time?" because its short cycle makes the fix highly visible.)

**[Upstream context — refutes a hypothesis]** Multi-gen acetate lineage does NOT monotonically equilibrate toward declared τ=136. The 5-gen run cycled 42 / 81 / 61 / 82 / 60 min — no trend. Why acetate, not glycerol, for this test — acetate is where the observed-vs-declared gap is most documented (~80 min observed vs 136 min declared on the canonical cache), inherited from wcEcoli. The candidate hypothesis was that the gap is a transient ParCa-initial-state artefact that multi-generation propagation would wash out. It isn't. Implication — the gap is mechanistically caused (FBA over-supplies amino acids early in gen → tRNAs stay 97-98% charged → SpoT degrades ppGpp → ribosomes elongate at 21 aa/s → mass triples in 80 min → D-period trigger fires early). This is the wcEcoli-inherited problem the chromosome-cycle-calibration investigation surfaces; it does NOT block the glycerol Stage 1 baseline from being delivered (F-S1), and it does NOT block Study 1 (DnaA expression dynamics, parameter-only on top of Study 0). Its proposed closure lives at Study 4 (mechanistic initiation, replacing the inherited Donachie / initiation-mass trigger).

Purpose

Question. Does v2ecoli, with the Stage 1 parameter overrides applied to a glycerol cache, produce a stable single-generation cell cycle near τ=150 min with the prescribed C=70 / D=30 timing, DnaA TE=1, DnaA fully stable, and the canonical chromosomal locus locations? Separately — does the v2ecoli refactor reproduce vEcoli's behaviour on the established acetate reference condition?
Mechanism / Model change. Parameter-only changes; no new processes. Pre-ParCa overrides via `studies/stage1_sanity_check/overrides.py` mutate raw_data at module load (replisome_rate=552.58 nt/s → C=70 min, dnaA TE=1.0, DARS1+DARS2 window widening, DATA-locus row). Post-ParCa knobs in `scripts/build_cache.py` apply D period, dnaA transcription scale, dnaA stability, condition selection. The new MIX0-845 media row and glycerol `condition_defs.tsv` entry register the τ=150 ABT-minimal-glycerol condition.
Expected outcome. **Deliverable** — glycerol Stage 1 single-gen cycle within ±20% of declared τ=150 min, all six Stage 1 parameter overrides apply cleanly on the glycerol cache. **Diagnostic** — acetate single-gen tracks vEcoli within 15% (confirms the cross-codebase equivalence). **Hypothesis test** — acetate 5-gen lineage does NOT monotonically converge to τ=136 (refutes the multi-gen-washout hypothesis; documents the upstream FBA over-supply gap whose proposed closure lives at Study 4, mechanistic initiation).
