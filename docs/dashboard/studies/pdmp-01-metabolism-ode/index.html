Main expert question
Purpose
Question. Can the Millard et al. 2017 kinetic ODE of E. coli central carbon and energy metabolism, wrapped in a linear-quadratic regulator (LQR) outer control layer for growth-rate regulation, replace v2ecoli's multi-objective FBA metabolism (biomass + homeostatic + kinetic-constraint, via `wholecell.utils.modular_fba`) while reproducing the Phase 0 interface statistics within tolerance across ≥3 environmental conditions?

Millard 2017 ATP-drain perturbation experiment: steady-state baseline (t=0–500s) → ATP cut to 30% of steady state at t=500s → recovery transient. 4-panel matplotlib chart: nucleotides (ATP/ADP/AMP), glycolysis intermediates (G6P/F6P/FDP/GAP/PEP/PYR), TCA cycle (CIT/ICIT/AKG/SUCCOA/FUM/MAL/OAA), and redox cofactors (NAD/NADH/NADP/NADPH). Demonstrates the Millard model recovers ATP to within 1% of steady-state within ~50 s after a 70% drain — fast because of the tight coupling between glycolytic flux and adenylate energy charge.
