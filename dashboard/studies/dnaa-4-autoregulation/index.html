◆ F-05 other draft literature
DnaA-ATP-vs-DnaA-ADP noise asymmetry (Haochen 2026-06-14): the DnaA-ADP curve is much SMOOTHER than DnaA-ATP even though ADP is downstream of ATP (ATP hydrolyses to ADP), so ADP should inherit ATP's noise. MECHANISM (where the asymmetry comes from): in flat_overrides/equilibrium_reaction_rates.tsv, DnaA-ATP formation (MONOMER0-160_RXN, apo+ATP<->DnaA-ATP) has integrate_dt=false (FAST-EQUILIBRIUM — re-solved to steady state every tick, so it instantly tracks the stochastic free-ATP/apo pools => noisy), while DnaA-ADP formation (MONOMER0-4565_RXN) and the hydrolysis (DNAA-INTRINSIC-HYDROLYSIS-RXN) are integrate_dt=true (KINETIC ODE integration over dt, which averages out the sub-tick stochasticity => smooth). IMPORTANT — this is NOT a bug: the fast-equilibrium treatment of DnaA+ATP<->DnaA-ATP is a DELIBERATE, physically-justified modeling choice (Haochen: cellular ATP is abundant, so DnaA-ATP binding equilibrates fast — fast-equilibrium is the appropriate approximation) and is the ORIGINAL whole-cell-model treatment. So the smoothness asymmetry is an EXPECTED consequence of that justified design, not an artifact to "correct". EXPERIMENT (Haochen/Eran endorsed "try this too") — RUN AND RESOLVED 2026-06-14: set MONOMER0-160_RXN integrate_dt false->true (kinetic integration of DnaA-ATP formation) to TEST whether the DnaA-ATP curve becomes smoother. Method: rather than a full-ParCa rebuild (which independently fails at the unrelated promoter-binding cvxpy solver, promoter_fitting.py:548 — NOT caused by this flag), the integrate_dt_mask was patched directly in a copy of the canonical cache (cache_dnaa4_autoreg, which already had ADP+hydrolysis kinetic), flipping ONLY MONOMER0-160 to kinetic, then resumed from the seed-1 gen5 dill for gen 6 (s=0.7 Hill, V=1.5e-3, k_h=0.025; gen completed cleanly, tau=59 min, divided). RESULT — NEGATIVE: kinetic ATP did NOT smooth the DnaA-ATP curve. Tick-jitter/mean of the DnaA-ATP form went 0.0082 (fast-eq) -> 0.0132 (kinetic, marginally WORSE), the ATP/ADP asymmetry ratio went 2.11 -> 2.41 (worse), and the ATP fraction shifted 0.327 -> 0.293. CONCLUSION: the asymmetry is NOT a solver-method artifact for ATP — it is a pool-size/topology effect. DnaA-ADP is the larger terminal sink (~278 vs ~132 molecules) that accumulates downstream, so its relative noise is intrinsically lower (~1/sqrt N buffering); DnaA-ATP is the smaller dynamic intermediate (formed by ATP binding, drained by hydrolysis + oriC/box turnover) and necessarily carries the turnover fluctuations. Noise DOES propagate ATP->ADP but is averaged out by the large ADP pool. DECISION (taken): REVERTED MONOMER0-160_RXN integrate_dt back to false — the justified, abundant-ATP, original-WCM fast-equilibrium default stands; the kinetic variant is not adopted (it doesn't help and is marginally worse). Comparison figure: out/dnaa4_atp_kinetic_vs_fasteq_gen6.png. (Residual box-binding free<->bound stochasticity in dnaa_box_binding.py is a separate, smaller noise source; reducing the visible ATP jitter further would mean enlarging/buffering the ATP pool or smoothing the box-binding turnover, NOT changing the equilibrium solver method.)
◆ F-06 other draft literature
Band-centering CLOSED via feedback strength (s/seed sweep, 2026-06-15): the dnaa-4 DnaA-pool-in-band test was PARTIAL at s=0.7 — seed 0's per-generation MEAN dipped out of [300,800] at g11-13 (246/187/269) while seeds 1,2 held. A focused sweep (RIDA/DDAH/DARS DISABLED — pure dnaa-4) resuming seed 0 from its gen-8 dill through the failing window g9-14 shows s=0.65 THREADS THE NEEDLE: every generation mean lands in band (409-545) AND the peak stays under 800 (781), converting the previously-failing gens to a clean per-gen PASS. s=0.60 also lifts the means in-band but the peak breaks 800 (827) — too weak. So s=0.65 (Hill K=0.5, n=4, V=1.5e-3, k_h=0.025) is the band-centering value: between s=0.7 (trough dips out) and s=0.6 (peak breaks band). HONEST caveats: (1) the test scores the generation-AVERAGE (operator generation_average_in_range) — at s=0.65 all gen-means are in-band, though the instantaneous trough still grazes ~251 momentarily; (2) this is seed 0 resumed through its dip window — a fully robust clean-pass claim is being confirmed by a 3-seed fresh 16-gen run at s=0.65 (launched 2026-06-15, RIDA/DARS off). DECISION: adopt s=0.65 as canonical (supersedes s=0.7), pending the 3-seed 16-gen confirmation. Runs: dnaa4_band_s065 / dnaa4_band_s060 (sweep); dnaa4_s065_seed{0,1,2}_16gen (confirmation).

Purpose

Question. Can DnaA negative autoregulation hold the DnaA pool self-stabilized in the literature [300,800] band across generations WITHOUT the manual V override (Mechanism A) — by wiring DnaA-ATP repression of its own promoter into the transcription recruitment (synth_prob = basal + delta_prob × dnaA-promoter occupancy) — and does this dissolve the dnaa-1/dnaa-2 V tension (0.8e-3 vs 1e-3) and the per-gen drift at their root?

Implementation requirements (6 items)

Concrete pieces of work that gate this study from Design → Build. Each generates a task.

REQ-1 — DnaA-ATP → dnaA-promoter autorepression edge [model]

Wire the negative recruitment edge so the dnaA synth prob is modulated down by dnaA-promoter occupancy; force the edge to survive the ParCa L1 promoter fit.

REQ-2 — Repression signal source [model]

Drive the bound-fraction from the adopted dnaA-promoter box occupancy (promoter_high_bound, option b) rather than the standard TF p_promoter_bound — confirmed to carry a usable signal (occupancy mean 1.41/2, range 0.5-2.0).

REQ-3 — Multinomial probability renormalization [model]

Renormalize the synth-prob distribution after scaling the dnaA TU, so the freed probability mass is not piled onto an arbitrary promoter (a review caught this).

REQ-4 — Hill-form calibration of the feedback strength [analysis]

Calibrate s / K / n so the feedback equilibrium lands DnaA in-band at a stable V; s=0.7, K=0.5, n=4 selected over linear (over-represses trough) and s=0.6/0.8.

REQ-5 — Baseline allocator over-draft robustness (#209) [infra]

PROTON NegativeCountsError on seed1 (allocator over-draft after an FBA infeasibility tick) fixed by resolve_overdraft clamp+warn in v2ecoli

REQ-6 — Resume-from-dill stability probe [tooling]

The --resume-dill path produces a daughter that never re-initiates replication (dill-roundtrip bug, docs/resume_dill_replication_init_bug.md); the continuous 16-gen runs supply the multi-generation stability evidence instead.
