Claim

Configured with operating conditions matched to the 2025 Palsson lab high-density E. coli study, does the v2ecoli ↔ pbg-bioreactordesign coupled model **render a structured comparison report** (sim-vs- published overlays + categorized divergences + open questions) on the published WT batch-phase prefix trajectories, suitable to feed mbp-06's gap-analysis synthesis? Investigation-level note: the original question framing ("match within tolerance") was reframed 2026-05-26 to "render a structured comparison." The success definition is whether the comparison was EXECUTED AND REPORTED COHERENTLY, not whether biology matched. All biological divergences feed mbp-06 unchanged.

Benchmark: Beulig et al. 2025 (mSystems, doi:10.1128/msystems.00323-25; Palsson lab), two-stage batch->fed-batch to 50-80 gDW/L. mbp-05 scopes to WT, BATCH-PHASE PREFIX (Beulig batch peak ~9.6 g/L). Compared surfaces: OD600/biomass, glucose, acetate + mixed-acid byproducts (lactate/formate/ethanol/pyruvate/succinate), GUR, OTR/CTR, base addition. v2ecoli's calibration (slow-growth M9, single cell) is OUTSIDE the high-density envelope — divergences are EXPECTED outputs categorized for mbp-06, not failures.

req-2-comparison-harness — Generic sim-vs-published comparison harness S [tooling]

Iterates over the study's `comparison_overlays` list, for each entry: - Resolves `sim_path` against the run output (handles `aggregation: per_biomass` and any other reductions). - Loads `published_csv` and extracts `published_column`. - Renders an overlay panel (sim trajectory + published trajectory, time-aligned) → report/overlay_<name>.png - Computes per-overlay derived metrics (end-of-run value diff, shape-similarity, peak timing, etc. — keyed by the panel's `backs_tests`). - Categorizes any large divergences into the four-axis taxonomy in report/divergence_triage.md (with evidence pointer to the panel + sim_path + published_column). The harness is GENERIC: adding a new comparison surface = adding one `comparison_overlays` entry. No code change. Future investigations reuse the harness with their own `comparison_overlays` list. Framework-layer (chris_feedback_2026_05_26 §2): hoisting `comparison_overlays` into pbg-superpowers schema is a deferred-upstream candidate (tracked in mbp-06 axis-E gap entry).

Plan & assumptions

Pipeline gate

Each study is a model-building gate: it answers "Can we safely build the next layer?"

Prerequisites: none (root study)
Proceed when: All comparison overlay panels rendered (one per `comparison_overlays` entry); report.md committed with `## Divergence triage` section populated against the four-category taxonomy. Divergences themselves are PASSED to mbp-06 as input — they do not gate this phase. The `divergences-categorized-in-report-card` test is the load-bearing one (per chris_feedback_2026_05_26 §10).

Key assumptions

  • Published trajectories are digitizable / available at sufficient resolution to overlay.
  • OD600 ↔ gDW conversion factor (od_to_gdw, default 0.34 — Beulig 2025 value; was textbook 0.33; matches mbp-02 default) holds at Palsson's cell-density regime, or is refit from the paper.
  • If Palsson is fed-batch only, this study scopes to the batch-phase prefix until pbg-bioreactordesign gains fed-batch (deferred enhancement).
  • 0D well-mixed assumption holds at the paper's reactor scale (bench).

Limitations

What this study explicitly does NOT validate (prevents overclaim).

  • EVALUATION PHASE (chris_feedback_2026_05_26 §10): mbp-05 deliberately does NOT make tolerance-based biology claims. The original framing (`end-of-run-od-matches-published` etc.) structurally encoded an open-ended menu of model knobs to turn — even with the 'no silent tuning' discipline, the impulse to tune to a tolerance-test was built into the test design. The reframe shifts the bound on the AI agent to 'execute and report'.
  • Single benchmark study; not a multi-dataset validation.
  • Fed-batch operations remain a deferred enhancement (pbg-bioreactordesign has no fed-batch yet); this study scopes to the BATCH-PHASE PREFIX of the Beulig dataset.
  • v2ecoli's calibration is slow-growth M9-glucose (~60-min doubling, single cell); the high-density 50–80 gDW/L regime is OUTSIDE the calibration envelope. Under the new framing this is no longer a 'tolerance must reflect this' caveat — it becomes one of the divergences mbp-06's gap analysis categorizes.
  • Strain scope: WT BW25113 only. SGKO (31 knockouts) / TRP / TRPp / MEL are deferred — natural candidate-future-studies under v2ecoli's variant scope.
  • Transcriptional-state matching against >470 Beulig transcriptomes is a unique benchmark axis (v2ecoli has transcripts; upstream dFBA path does not) — deferred to mbp-09 (pre-seeded in mbp-06).
