Jump initiation rates — discrete vs Poisson (real run)

Total transcription-initiation events per tick over a 300 s v2ecoli baseline run, discrete vs. the per-promoter/per-protein Poisson tau-leap sampler. Both modes track the same rising trend (initiation grows as the cell accumulates gene copies; discrete mean 49.4, sd 9.7), but the Poisson sampler adds substantial per-tick stochasticity on top (mean 50.4, sd 12.2, range 15-77) — the continuous-time jump signature. Yet aggregate endpoints are conserved: cell_mass 1350.4->1350.9 fg (Δ+0.036%), dry_mass 405.3->405.5 fg (Δ+0.043%) — the consumption-matched homeostat washes per-tick jump noise out at the mass level.
