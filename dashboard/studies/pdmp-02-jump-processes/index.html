✓ jump-variance-lives-at-count-level computational confirms literature
Empirical confirmation of the Phase-2 redirect: across 4 seeds at 250 steps, every AGGREGATE observable (cell_mass, dry_mass, total monomer, ATP[c] count) washes out to ~0.028% cross-seed CV, while the DIRECT jump-event count (cumulative transcription-initiation events, rnap_data.rna_init_event) shows 0.85% CV -- ~30x higher. So the jump-process stochasticity is real but lives at the per-event count level, not in aggregate mass/pool observables the consumption_matched homeostat regulates -- which is exactly why Phase-3 inference must anchor on count-level listeners.
observed: CV: cell_mass 0.0285%, dry_mass 0.0288%, monomer 0.0273%, ATP 0.0283%, cumulative rna_init 0.846% (~30x) · in run baseline N=4 x 250 steps tick-by-tick (scripts/phase2_count_variance.py)

Discovery implications

Where this study's results leave the mechanism model — and what to investigate next.

✓ Resolved uncertainties

  • Continuous-time per-promoter / per-protein Poisson tau-leap samplers were implemented and land in `poisson` mode. The "150 fg" headline is max |Delta cell_mass| = 150 fg between configs — a transient on a single trajectory pair, computed in scripts/trajectory_divergence.py on the cell_mass quantity (NOT measured at the rnap_data listener, which is a narrative literal absent from the computing script); and it cuts against this study's own thesis that cell_mass is the wrong observable.
  • cell_mass is decisively the WRONG observable for jump-process variance: the consumption_matched homeostat (from Phase 1) washes per-tick variance out of cell_mass by construction. Three controller- tuning attempts (tau-EMA, open-loop, sparse injection) all failed to recover mass variance.

● Remaining uncertainties

  • The original Phase-2 gate `pdmp-trajectory-distribution-match` — trajectory-distribution W2 match to the v2ecoli baseline on cell_mass across all Phase-0 conditions — FAILED: the Poisson cell_mass W2 grows monotonically 9.73 → 15.35 → 49.46. Per the closeout this is the WRONG gate (cell_mass is the wrong observable), a legitimate negative/structural result — but the gate status is FAILED, not passed/accepted. A replacement count-level trajectory-distribution criterion has not yet been formally specified or passed.
  • The Anderson-Darling Exp(1) exponential-clock verification (>=1e4 inter-event intervals) and the OU first-passage division-trigger validation (KS < 0.02) remain unrun.

Alternate hypotheses (1)

It is the consumption_matched homeostat specifically (not jump sampling per se) that erases per-tick variance; loosening or disabling the homeostat would restore cell_mass as a usable variance observable.
The homeostat actively tracks a reference growth trajectory, so any per-tick fluctuation it sees is corrected within a tick.
Mechanism elements: consumption_matched-driver, cell_mass-observable, jump-variance-propagation
Discriminating observables: cell_mass CV with the homeostat on vs off under identical jump seeds, count-listener variance (rnap_data, ribosome_data) vs cell_mass variance

Mechanism update proposals (1)

inference-observable (cell_mass -> count-level listeners) revise needs expert approval
Anchor all downstream variance-based inference on count-level listeners (rnap_data, ribosome_data, monomer_counts) rather than cell_mass; the homeostat makes mass variance-blind. This redirected Phase 3.
