v2ecoli neural-network surrogate

A pbg-torch residual-MLP surrogate trained on baseline rollouts, predicting the per-step delta of 8 observables across growth/mass, exchange fluxes, metabolic fluxes, protein and transcript abundance, and chromosome state. One whole trajectory was held out for evaluation.

Performance: surrogate 39,009 steps/s vs. simulator 14.25 steps/s  →  2,737× speedup (over 8 observables).
