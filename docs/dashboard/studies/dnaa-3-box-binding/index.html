Purpose
Question. With DnaA binding to its 315 boxes (4 affinity pools) in fast equilibrium on top of the dnaa-2 baseline, does the cell cycle stay unchanged (8/8 divisions, oriC 1↔2, 0 re-init) and the DnaA-ATP fraction stay in the [0.2,0.5] band — i.e. does adding the read-only binding observer leave the validated dnaa-2 behaviour intact while providing the bound/free partitioning later studies need?
Mechanism / Model change. DnaA binds 315 boxes in four pools, each a fast equilibrium governed only by K_d (absolute k_fwd/k_rev are degenerate after the steady-state solve): - chromosomal high-affinity (302 sites): K_d ≈ 1 nM, binds DnaA-ATP OR DnaA-ADP - oriC high-affinity (3 sites: R1, R2, R4): K_d ≈ 1 nM, binds DnaA-ATP OR DnaA-ADP - oriC low-affinity (8 sites): K_d ≈ 100 nM, DnaA-ATP ONLY - dnaA-promoter high-affinity (2 sites: box 1, box 2): K_d ≈ 1 nM, ATP OR ADP High-affinity pools track bound-ATP and bound-ADP SEPARATELY (3 counts: free / bound-ATP / bound-ADP) so the correct nucleotide form can be released in later studies. The low-affinity pool tracks 2 counts (free / bound-ATP). DNA binding does NOT alter the DnaA-ATP intrinsic hydrolysis rate (bf8b82e k≈0.046/min applies uniformly to bound and free DnaA-ATP).
Expected outcome. The dnaa-2 cell cycle is preserved (8/8 divisions, oriC 1↔2, 0 re-init) and the DnaA-ATP fraction stays in [0.2,0.5]. The chromosomal high-affinity pool (302 sites) is the dominant binding pool. Box count doubles across replication (315 at birth → 630 at termination → 315 per daughter).

Key assumptions
  • All 307 consensus DnaA boxes (TTWTNCACA) are high-affinity (K_d ≈ 1 nM).
  • oriC has 3 high-affinity + 8 low-affinity boxes; low-affinity K_d ≈ 100 nM.
  • DnaA-ATP and DnaA-ADP binding to boxes is FAST EQUILIBRIUM (only K_d matters; absolute k_fwd/k_rev magnitudes degenerate after the steady-state solve).
  • DNA binding does NOT alter the DnaA-ATP intrinsic hydrolysis rate (bf8b82e k ≈ 0.046/min applies uniformly to bound and free DnaA-ATP).
  • Higher-order interactions between low-affinity sites are DEFERRED to a later study.
  • High-affinity pools track bound-ATP and bound-ADP separately so the correct nucleotide form can be released later by downstream regulatory studies (extrinsic DnaA-ATP/ADP conversion + sequestration; per Haochen's spec).
  • DISCREPANCY (recorded): the Stage-1 parameter brief lists oriC low-affinity K_d as a 50→1 nM range; dnaa-3 uses Haochen's first-step K_d ≈ 100 nM, with higher-order site interactions deferred to a later study. The spec assumption governs.
