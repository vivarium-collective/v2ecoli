Per-TU initiation distribution — discrete vs Poisson (real run)

Distribution of cumulative transcription-initiation counts across all 3277 transcription units, discrete vs. Poisson tau-leap. Both modes produce the same per-TU mean (4.52 vs 4.61 initiations/TU) and the same heavy-tailed shape (log y-axis), confirming the Poisson sampler reproduces the discrete model's per-gene initiation statistics, not just the aggregate rate.
