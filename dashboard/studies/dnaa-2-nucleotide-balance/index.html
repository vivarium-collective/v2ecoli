Main insight ADOPTED Haochen's mechanism (commit bf8b82e). The naive model pinned DnaA-ATP at ~0.997 because v2ecoli's ecoli-equilibrium integrates EVERY reaction to steady state each tick — including DnaA-ADP binding — so it instantly reverts hydrolysis-made DnaA-ADP back to apo → DnaA-ATP. Haochen's diagnosis: DnaA-ADP dissociation is SLOW (k_r ≈ 1×10⁻⁷/s ≪ k_hyd ≈ 1×10⁻⁴/s) and NOT at equilibrium. His fix keeps the DnaA-ADP reaction BIDIRECTIONAL but flags it integrate_dt=true (integrated kinetically over the timestep), and adds DnaA-ATP intrinsic hydrolysis as a one-way kinetic reaction in the same equilibrium machinery. With this, his LITERAL rate constants — no tuning, no override — land the DnaA-ATP fraction in [0.2, 0.5]. The mechanism is the lever; no K_d / [ATP]:[ADP] / expected-fraction revision is needed. — computed from findings

Question.
