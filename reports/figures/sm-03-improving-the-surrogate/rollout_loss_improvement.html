Can we do better? Rollout-loss training

sm-01 diagnosed the neural net's failure as autoregressive instability (one-step training never penalizes compounding error). The principled fix: train the net on its own multi-step unrolled predictions.

Follow-up finding: multi-step rollout-loss training genuinely helps — it eliminates the instability (0/8 unstable vs 1/8 for one-step) and improves rollout accuracy (0.053 vs 0.069). But it still does not beat the linear baseline (0.019). The right training technique narrows the gap; for a target this smooth, a linear model remains the honest choice. Rollout-loss is the technique to carry to harder, genuinely nonlinear targets — where a linear model would fail.
