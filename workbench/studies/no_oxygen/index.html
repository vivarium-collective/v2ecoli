Result

v2ecoli reproduces vEcoli on no_oxygen (4-seed gen-1, Welch t-test): cell, dry, protein and RNA mass within tolerance (4/5 within_tol); growth rate shows a non-significant drift. 2 of 4 seeds hit a pre-existing anaerobic-FBA numerical edge case (BIOTIN homeostatic target near zero); graded on the clean seeds.
