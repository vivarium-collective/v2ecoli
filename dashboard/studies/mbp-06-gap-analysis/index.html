Biology — what this study is about

Question & approach

Question. After mbp-01..05 have produced their per-study findings, what are the remaining gaps (in v2ecoli's biology coverage, the coupling layer, the pbg-bioreactordesign physics, and the Beulig benchmark scope) that prevent the investigation from answering its overarching question, and how should those gaps be prioritized as the next investigation iteration (mbp-* round 2, or a sibling investigation)?
Hypothesis / Expected outcome. A structured findings document (findings.md + this study's `findings.entries[]`, populated post-impl) that enumerates ≥ 8 named gaps across the five axes (with per-axis floors: ≥ 3 on axes A and D based on prior known limitations), each with a concrete candidate_resolution AND with the extended schema fields below (chris_feedback_2026_05_26 §11 concern 2): - resolution_type: model_improvement | scope_decision | upstream_PR | expert_elicitation | further_investigation - resolution_venue: <repo / sibling investigation / program-level decision> - decision_authority: <who decides — especially for scope_decision> - provenance: this_investigation | carried_prior | external_input | expert_elicitation - priority: 1 (top) | 2 | 3 - evidence: [list of upstream test results / limitations / open_questions / mandatory_findings_to_consider entries] Priorities are assigned INDEPENDENTLY of provenance — a carried prior can be priority 1 even if mbp-05 didn't surface it fresh. The document feeds the next investigation cycle: roadmap-revision PR on references/expert/multiscale_bioprocess_roadmap.md (if upstream) + new study scaffolds for the prioritized gaps.
Mechanism / Model change. No new simulation. The study WALKS the prior-study artifacts: every behavior_test's outcome, every recorded finding, every limitation / open_question / candidate-future-study pointer. It synthesizes these into a structured gap-analysis covering five named axes: A. **v2ecoli biology coverage** — places where v2ecoli's metabolism / regulation / mass machinery is insufficient for the reactor regime (e.g. low-DO acetate overflow strength, high-density maintenance re-allocation per Beulig 2025). B. **Coupling layer** — limitations of the v2ecoli ↔ pbg-bioreactor- design adapter (biomass-ownership disable, timestep mismatch, single-cell-with-Division ceiling). C. **pbg-bioreactordesign physics** — what BiRD doesn't yet model (fed-batch operations, multi-stream feeds, pH-CO2 coupling). D. **Beulig benchmark scope** — what was deferred from mbp-05 (SGKO / TRP / MEL strain extension, transcriptional-state matching against >470 transcriptomes, fed-batch phases I–IV). E. **Methodology** — places where the biological-BDD discipline (claims → tests → triage) didn't catch a gap soon enough, or where the cell-side interface contract needs revision based on what was learned. For each gap, the study records: signature, evidence (which test(s) / finding(s) surfaced it), severity (blocks-next-step | reduces-coverage | open-question), candidate-resolution (a new study slug, an upstream PR to pbg-bioreactordesign, an expert elicitation), and priority (1–3).
