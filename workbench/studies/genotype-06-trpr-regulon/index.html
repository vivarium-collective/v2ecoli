Q When the perturbed gene is a transcriptional regulator rather than an enzyme, how does v2ecoli realize the knockout, and how much of that regulator's documented biology can the model represent at all? Concretely, for trpR: by which routes can the genotype be built, what does each route let us observe, and what does the model's own regulatory parameterization put out of reach of every route?

Claim

A regulator knockout is realizable by three distinct routes in v2ecoli, and the route determines what can be observed. For trpR the model's regulon is structurally complete - 12 edges spanning all 5 of EcoCyc's operator regions - so the divergence from biology is one of parameterization, not coverage: the TrpR repression terms are 2.62-4.03x where the literature reports 70-300x, and on every gene where both mechanisms act the model's attenuation term exceeds its repression term, inverting the documented ordering. The model's mechanistic reach over transcriptional regulation overall is ~51% of mRNA cistrons.

TrpR is the tryptophan repressor. Bound to L-tryptophan it forms a holorepressor that occupies operators in front of the tryptophan biosynthetic operon and several other loci, shutting transcription down when tryptophan is plentiful. Removing trpR therefore derepresses tryptophan biosynthesis - it is one of the cleanest and best characterised regulatory knockouts in E. coli, which is why it is a good public exemplar even when the motivation for studying it comes from elsewhere. Two mechanisms control the trp operon, and their relative strength is the part that matters here. TrpR repression is the coarse, dominant switch, reported at 70-300 fold. Transcriptional attenuation - a translation-coupled decision made at a leader peptide - modulates on top of it at roughly 8-10 fold. Repression dominates; attenuation trims. v2ecoli represents transcriptional regulation as a table of fold-change terms keyed by transcription-factor common name. That representation is what bounds this study: a regulator can only act on genes it has a row for. For trpR the model carries twelve rows, covering all five EcoCyc operator regions - the trp operon (trpLEDCBA), aroH, aroL, mtr, and trpR's own autoregulation - plus aroM and yaiA. Every documented region is represented; each is repressed by trpR in the merged fold_changes + fold_changes_nca table the model actually loads. So a trpR knockout in v2ecoli is not a perturbation of a smaller object - the regulon is structurally complete against EcoCyc. What bounds it is parameterization, not coverage: whichever route builds the genotype, the response is confined to the magnitudes the model's twelve edges carry, and those magnitudes (2.62-4.03x) are an order of magnitude below the literature's 70-300x. That is a statement about the model's regulatory parameterization, and it is visible without running anything.

Test

Read the model's shipped regulatory tables directly (fold_changes.tsv filtered by fold_changes_removed.tsv, transcriptional_attenuation.tsv, genes.tsv, rnas.tsv) to fix what the model can represent before anything is run, then realize the ΔtrpR genotype by each available route and record what each route does. Route A builds the deletion at the ParCa level; route B zeroes the TrpR monomer's translation efficiency on a post-ParCa run. A third route - imposing a measured differential-expression table at ParCa time via the rnaseq_experimental key - exists in the model and is named here for completeness, but is not exercised by this study: it requires a measured DE table, which makes it an input question rather than a property of the genotype machinery.

Result

The static pass is complete (F-01 to F-04, measured from the model's own tables and holding independently of any simulation). ROUTE A HAS RUN: both arms fail ParCa at step 9 on the TRP[c] amino-acid homeostasis check, with every structural build-integrity axis passing first (F-05, F-06). ROUTE B HAS ALSO RUN (F-07, F-08): removing TrpR derepresses the trp operon 6.29x in AA-replete media - where the model declares TrpR active - and 1.06x in basal, where it declares it inactive, confirming the regulation works and is bounded to within a small factor of the model's own 2.62-4.03x parameterization rather than the literature's 70-300x. The route comparison itself - what each build route can and cannot represent - is the deliverable, not a preliminary to one.

Removing TrpR in the condition where the model declares it active derepresses the trp operon 6.29x; in basal, where the model declares it inactive, the same knockout changes nothing (1.06x). Plan & assumptions

Pipeline gate

Each study is a model-building gate: it answers "Can we safely build the next layer?"

Prerequisites: none (root study)
Proceed when: The static regulatory-reach pass (F-01..F-04) is complete and re-derivable. The run arms may proceed independently; nothing downstream waits on them.

Key assumptions

  • TrpR represses the trp operon 70-300 fold in vivo; transcriptional attenuation contributes a further 8-10 fold, and repression is the dominant of the two.
  • EcoCyc documents five TrpR operator-bearing regions - trpLEDCBA, aroH, aroL, mtr, and trpR's own autoregulation.
  • tnaA (tryptophanase) is the main catabolic sink for tryptophan, and tnaB - a second Trp permease - shares its operon. A trpR-only arm is therefore not like-for-like against a ΔtrpR ΔtnaA reference strain.

Limitations

What this study explicitly does NOT validate (prevents overclaim).

  • The static findings (F-01..F-04) measure the model's PARAMETER TABLES. They constrain what a run can show but are not themselves run results. F-05..F-08 are the run results: route A (build) and route B (multigen) have both executed.
  • Route C - imposing a measured differential-expression table at ParCa time via the rnaseq_experimental key - is named but not exercised. It depends on which DE table is supplied, which makes it an input question rather than a property of the machinery.
  • F-07's positive arm is measured in with_aa, which is a blunt instrument for the question. Relative to basal it supplies all 20 amino acids, shortens the doubling time from 44.0 to 25.0 min, and declares five TFs active and three inactive rather than TrpR alone (condition_defs.tsv). The 6.29x is therefore attributable to "a condition where the model declares TrpR active", not to tryptophan specifically. The basal_with_trp condition (ecoli-sources#15, merged 2026-08-23) isolates tryptophan at basal's own 44.0 min and is the intended clean re-run; it is not yet reachable from v2ecoli, which still pins ecoli-sources to a rev predating it.
  • EcoCyc region membership is entered by hand in the evidence extractor. It is literature, not model data, and is the one input a reader must take on trust rather than re-derive.

Discovery implications

Where this study's results leave the mechanism model — and what to investigate next.

✓ Resolved uncertainties

  • Whether a ParCa-level TF knockout prunes that TF's regulon entries. It does NOT - the deletion transform touches genes, transcription_units, dna_sites and the genome sequence, and never fold_changes. All 12 trpR rows survive a trpR deletion, so ParCa continues fitting delta_prob entries for a regulator no longer in the genome. This closes an open decision on the parent investigation.

● Remaining uncertainties

  • Whether the inverted table ordering (F-03) manifests as an inverted RUN response once attenuation is enabled, or whether downstream machinery re-weights the two mechanisms.
  • Whether ParCa's continued fitting of an absent regulator's regulon is a defect or a defensible modelling choice. It needs deciding for every TF knockout, not just trpR.
