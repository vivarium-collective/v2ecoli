Plan & assumptions

Key assumptions

  • A ParCa build is deterministic given its inputs, so the same genotype bundle reconstructs to the same sim_data. This is what lets a manifest hash serve as the genotype identity and lets builds be cached rather than repeated.
  • ★ The round-trip invariant must be TWO-BRANCH, and the obvious one-branch form is vacuous for the defect it most needs to catch. "Every retained feature's sequence is unchanged" is wrong twice: features straddling the deletion are SUPPOSED to lose those bases, and once that is relaxed the check still cannot see an off-by-one splice, because an off-by-one preserves every length. The working form asserts byte-identity only for features whose length is unchanged, and for shrunk features asserts the sequence equals the original with exactly the deleted span excised.
  • Coordinates in the EcoCyc-derived flat files are 1-based INCLUSIVE - a feature spanning [L, R] occupies genome_sequence[L-1:R]. Verified two ways: getter_functions.py:194 slices exactly that way, and of 174 '+'-strand genes 157 start with ATG under that reading versus 0 under the 0-based one. Load-bearing, because feature LENGTHS stay correct under an off-by-one while the sequence silently shifts.
  • A knockout that fails to build is a RESULT, not a failed study. For an essential gene the ParCa fit may not converge at all, which is a legitimate and informative outcome qualitatively unlike the translation-level knockout's. The tests below are written so that a non-converging fit is recorded rather than treated as infrastructure failure.

Limitations

What this study explicitly does NOT validate (prevents overclaim).

  • Establishes that the build is internally consistent, NOT that the resulting physiology is sensible. Everything about how the cell behaves is downstream and belongs to studies 01-06.
  • A single-genotype build integrity check does not establish the transform is correct in general. Genome-wide confidence needs the panel, and ultimately the tranche-B runner.
  • The AA-kcat axis has no band yet, so it is a recorded observation rather than a graded criterion on the first pass.
  • Run mode is load-bearing and the two tiers answer different questions. A fast-mode build (~104 s measured) fits 7 of 51 conditions and is sufficient for the structural readouts - sequence round-trip, chromosome length, feature inventory, and deleted-gene set membership were all measured on one. A full-mode build (several hours, per docs/generate_full_parca.md) fits all conditions and is required before any claim about FITTED PARAMETER VALUES, because the CLI's own --allow-partial-fit help flags step 9's mechanistic fits as failing on "numerically-marginal kinetics (e.g. debug mode's truncated TF set)" - the exact regime a fast build is in. So fast-mode kcat magnitudes are not trustworthy and must not be graded. The practical consequence is that this study can screen a genotype panel cheaply in fast mode and reserve full-mode builds for genotypes advancing to physiology. That is what makes a panel or a genome-wide screen tractable at all: the alternative prices every genotype at hours.
