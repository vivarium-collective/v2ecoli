v2ecoli neural-network surrogate

A pbg-torch residual-MLP surrogate trained on baseline rollouts, predicting the per-step delta of 11,569 observables across growth/mass, exchange fluxes, metabolic fluxes, protein and transcript abundance, and chromosome state. One whole trajectory was held out for evaluation.

Performance: surrogate 3,818 steps/s vs. simulator 14.38 steps/s  →  265× speedup (over 11,569 observables).
