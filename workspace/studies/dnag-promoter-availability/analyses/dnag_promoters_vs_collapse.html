Six seeds, medians shown. dnaG (red) and its 41 ribosomal-protein class-mates (green) lose promoter copies identically as the lineage stalls — 2 to 1, which is ordinary gene dosage as the doubling time stretches — yet dnaG’s transcription falls ~1e+05-fold while the peers’ rises. dnaG was never without a promoter on any of ~120,000 timesteps. Promoter availability accounts for 6.3% of the collapse in log space; the defect lies in the per-promoter probability.
