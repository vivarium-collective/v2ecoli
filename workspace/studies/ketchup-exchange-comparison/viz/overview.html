Results

v2ecoli ↔ Millard 2017 agree on the overflow axis: both run glucose-limited with no acetate secretion (✓ matches — but Millard only exchanges glucose + acetate, so the match is over 2 metabolites). Both KETCHUP core models diverge (✗ diverges): per 100 glucose they respire far harder (O₂ ≈ −135 vs v2ecoli −9; CO₂ ≈ +145 vs +33) and secrete acetate (≈ +71, absent in v2ecoli → 'appeared'/'lost'). The v2ecoli + Millard FBA-bridge (✗ diverges) sits in between on respiration (O₂ ≈ -26 per 100 glucose — ~3× the bare baseline’s −9, but still far below the KETCHUP cores’ −135) while keeping the baseline’s glucose-limited, acetate-free, CO₂-quiet exchange signature. So pinning v2ecoli’s central-carbon FBA to the Millard ODE nudges it toward the kinetic cores’ respiration without flipping it into acetate overflow.
