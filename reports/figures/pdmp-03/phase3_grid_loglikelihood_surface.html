Phase 3 — grid log-likelihood landscape (real forward runs)

Total log-likelihood (= polypeptide_init + transcript_init contributions, summed in log space) evaluated on the coarse 3x3 forward grid over the two ABC parameters translation_scale (ts) and protein_scale (ps), mean of 4 seeds per cell (±sd shown). The surface is monotone toward the ts=0.850, ps=0.850 corner (red box, -1063) — i.e. the 3x3 grid does NOT bracket the maximum-likelihood point; it lies at or beyond the low-scale corner, so a follow-up sweep must extend ts, ps below 0.85. This is the real forward-likelihood landscape, NOT the planned ABC-SMC posterior (which requires running the SMC sampler against synthetic data). Source: .pbg/runs/pdmp-03-abc-2d/ (9 cells x 4 seeds).
