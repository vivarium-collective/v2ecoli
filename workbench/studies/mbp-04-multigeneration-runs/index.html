Claim

Does the mbp-03 coupled composite (v2ecoli ↔ pbg-bioreactordesign) stay mass-conserving, computationally tractable, and physically coherent over multiple v2ecoli generations (3+ doublings) under sustained reactor dynamics — capturing the regime transition from glucose-replete exponential growth into glucose-limited or DO-limited slowdown as the population grows and consumes the batch?

Carbon ledger: glucose-C in -> biomass-C (~0.46 gC/gDW) + CO2-C (~half of aerobic glucose-C; omitting it is a guaranteed ~50% failure) + acetate/lactate/formate/ethanol/pyruvate/succinate-C (aerobic overflow per Vemuri 2006). Nitrogen ledger (narrow): NH4+ in -> biomass-N (~0.12 gN/gDW). Both residuals expected |<0.02| at every step. Headline observable is the growth->limitation regime transition.

Pure extension of mbp-03 — no new physics, just longer runs and a mass-balance diagnostic Step. Fed-batch is OUT of scope here; this study validates the BATCH regime over multiple generations. Population sparsity (chris_feedback_2026_05_26 §10 mbp-04 specifics): mbp-04 inherits the cells_per_agent scaling from mbp-02 (Eran's choice 2026-05-26). Production runs of three-generation-batch should set cells_per_agent such that initial biomass concentration is in the glucose-depletion-feasible range (target ~0.01-0.1 gDW/L initial so 4 g/L glucose depletes within the 240-min sim window). At literal-sum (cells_per_agent=1.0), the population cannot deplete a 4 g/L pool in 240 min — the regime-transition test premise wouldn't resolve. See open_question glucose-pool-sizing for the calibration discipline.

Implementation requirements (1 items)

Concrete pieces of work that gate this study from Design → Build. Each generates a task.

req-1-mass-balance-auditor — MassBalanceAuditor Step S [process]

PBG Step that integrates external-pool decrements, biomass accumulation, metabolism exchange-flux outputs, and gas-phase transfer (via the BiRD coupler diagnostics) to compute carbon/nitrogen mass-balance residuals. Reads cumulative trackers exposed by EnvironmentDriver and PopulationAggregator. Writes reactor.diagnostics.{carbon,nitrogen}_residual. **EXPLICIT required-tracked species (chris_feedback_2026_05_26 §9 — must be in the auditor design, not just in failure_modes as candidate causes):** Carbon ledger (target tolerance 2% — only tractable if CO2 + acetate explicitly bookkept): - Glucose decrement (external pool, via EnvironmentDriver cumulative tracker) - Biomass carbon accumulation (∫ μ × biomass × C_coefficient × dt; C_coefficient ≈ 0.46 gC/gDW for E. coli) - **CO2-C** evolved by metabolism (∫ metabolism CO2 exchange flux × biomass × dt; flows out via gas-liquid transfer per BiRD coupler). Roughly half of glucose-C consumed under aerobic growth; missing it is a guaranteed 50% C-balance failure, NOT a small error mode. - **Acetate-C** and other byproduct organic acids (∫ metabolism ACET/LAC/FOR/PYR/SUCC exchange fluxes × biomass × dt). v2ecoli produces acetate under aerobic overflow per Vemuri 2006; the C-balance must track it. Nitrogen ledger (target tolerance 2% — pool defined narrowly so the tolerance is achievable): - **N-pool definition:** NH4+ exchange flux + biomass-N. EXCLUDES intermediate amino acids / peptides / nucleotides (which sum to << 2% under steady-state turnover). - NH4+ decrement (external pool, via EnvironmentDriver) - Biomass nitrogen accumulation (∫ μ × biomass × N_coefficient × dt; N_coefficient ≈ 0.12 gN/gDW for E. coli) Residual computation: carbon_residual = (Σ inputs - Σ outputs - Δ biomass-C) / Σ inputs nitrogen_residual = (Σ inputs - Σ outputs - Δ biomass-N) / Σ inputs Both should be |residual| < 0.02 at every emit step over the run.

Plan & assumptions

Pipeline gate

Each study is a model-building gate: it answers "Can we safely build the next layer?"

Prerequisites: none (root study)
Proceed when: Regime-transition test passes (population growth flattens once a limiting substrate is exhausted); mass-balance audits close; wall-clock budget met; no NaN / divergence.

Key assumptions

  • Batch mode only; no feed addition (pbg-bioreactordesign has no fed-batch support yet).
  • Initial glucose 4 g/L is enough for ~3 generations at v2ecoli's yield; refine with calibration.
  • v2ecoli's metabolism remains stable in glucose-limited regime (no runaway / NaN). If not, that becomes a finding for this phase.
  • Wall-clock budget of 30 min for 240-min sim is a placeholder; the spec PR sets the real budget.

Limitations

What this study explicitly does NOT validate (prevents overclaim).

  • Batch only; fed-batch operations require either an upstream pbg-bioreactordesign enhancement or a v2ecoli-side feed Step — deferred (candidate-future-study; see mbp-06).
  • Single seed; stochastic variation across seeds not characterized here (a candidate-future-study). Cells_per_agent scaling further washes per-seed stochastic variance — explicit cost of the mbp-02 architectural choice.
  • Bubble column geometry; stirred tank / airlift not validated.
  • Acetate (+ lactate / formate / ethanol / pyruvate / succinate) handling depends on v2ecoli's metabolism; the MassBalanceAuditor explicitly tracks each species (req-1) but biological correctness of the production rates is not separately validated this phase.
  • Population uses cells_per_agent scaling (mbp-02) to make 240-min batch dynamics resolvable. At scaling factors k ≥ ~1e6, per-cell stochastic variance washes out at the bulk mean — correct biology at high density, but obscures per-cell heterogeneity that may matter for Beulig's bistable-expression regimes. Surrogate engine remains the path for the full high-density regime (mbp-06).
