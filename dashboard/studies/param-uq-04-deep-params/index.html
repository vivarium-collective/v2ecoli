Key assumptions
  • The uqEcoli parameter bounds (RNAP fractions, FBA weights, mass fraction) are meaningful uncertainty ranges for v2ecoli. They may need re-calibration if v2ecoli's ParCa pipeline has shifted the baseline physiological state since the original uqEcoli work.
