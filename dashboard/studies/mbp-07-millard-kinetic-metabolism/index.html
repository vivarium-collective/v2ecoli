Question & approach

Question. The Millard kinetic-metabolism arc, end-to-end. Can the Millard 2017 kinetic central-carbon ODE replace v2ecoli's constraint-based central- carbon FBA as a drop-in cell engine under the cell-side interface contract? Three linked questions: 1. BUILD — does the swapped composite (baseline_millard) build and run, with non-trivial live central fluxes (kinetic ODE active, not FBA)? 2. VALIDATE — does the Millard whole-cell model recover growth comparable to the FBA baseline, and (once the Millard cell exposes mass-exchange accounting) close mass conservation? 3. COMPARE — coupled to the same BiRD reactor and run against the Beulig WT batch prefix, does the Millard kinetic central metabolism track the bioreactor (growth, substrate, gas transfer) BETTER than v2ecoli's WCM-FBA — and how do both compare to the iML1515 genome-scale FBA (Palsson)? Rendered as a structured 3-arm report-card comparison (per-group axis verdicts + categorized divergences), NOT a tolerance gate (execute-and-report, per chris_feedback_2026_05_26 §10).

Implementation requirements (3 items)

Concrete pieces of work that gate this study from Design → Build. Each generates a task.

req-1-millard-exchange-accounting — Millard cell exposes O2/CO2 exchange to the reactor coupler M [process]

The Millard cell (baseline_millard) replaced FBA metabolism, so it does NOT populate `agents.*.environment.exchange` with O2/CO2 — which is what ReactorCellCoupler reads to drive reactor consumption. Without this the Millard-coupled reactor is degenerate (cell consumes no reactor O2; DO stays at saturation). The Millard 2017 model HAS an O2 species + central fluxes, so translate Millard's O2 uptake / CO2 evolution (central_fluxes) into the cell- side exchange the coupler consumes (counts into environment.exchange, or a Millard-aware coupler input). This is the deferred Millard mass-exchange accounting (also unblocks the wcm-mass-conservation gate on the Millard cell).

Unblocks: the Millard arm + wcm-mass-conservation-closes-millard on baseline_millard

req-2-millard-coupled-composite — reactor_bird_coupled (Millard variant) S [composite]

A composite coupling baseline_millard (instead of baseline_population's FBA cell) to BiRDTransportHours + ReactorCellCoupler — i.e. the mbp-03 wiring with the Millard cell engine swapped in (the cell-side interface contract makes this a drop-in). Likely a `cell_engine` param on reactor_bird_coupled, or a sibling reactor_bird_coupled_millard.

req-3-beulig-report-card — Reuse the mbp-05 Beulig comparison harness for all arms M [harness]

Run the arms against the Beulig batch prefix through the mbp-05 comparison harness, producing a report_card_verdict.json per arm at the card paths below, so the report_card_axis tests grade them. Reuses mbp-05's comparison_overlays + grader (req: mbp-05 built). Now covers THREE arms — the Millard kinetic cell (millard_vs_beulig), the WCM-FBA cell (vs_beulig), and the iML1515 genome-scale FBA contrast (iml1515_vs_beulig).
