Main expert question
Purpose
Question. With DnaA box binding wired in, does v2ecoli show the expected two-step oriC occupancy pattern — chromosomal boxes fill first, then oriC fills once chromosome sites are saturated? Does free DnaA increase after titration completes, just before initiation?
Mechanism / Model change. 307 consensus DnaA boxes (TTWTNCACA) chromosome-wide, all high-affinity (Kd ~1 nM). oriC has 3 high-affinity + 8 low-affinity boxes (Kd ~100 nM low-affinity). dnaA promoter has 2 high-affinity + 2 low-affinity boxes. DnaA binding does NOT alter the DnaA-ATP hydrolysis rate from Study 2.
Expected outcome. Six expected behaviours per the plan PDF: (a) unoccupied chromosome sites decrease over time; (b) unoccupied oriC stays high until chromosome saturates; (c) once unoccupied chromosome sites are ~10 (within order-of-magnitude of unoccupied oriC), oriC binding begins from high-affinity sites; (d) free DnaA increases after titration; (e) oriC occupancy first low, then high — a 2-step process; (f) high-DnaA perturbation shows reduced dnaA promoter activity (autorepression check).

Key assumptions
  • All 307 consensus DnaA boxes are high-affinity Kd~1 nM. Refines if dnaA-stoichiometry mass-spec data (Chenli Liu's Nature Microbiology paper, cited in plan) supports more granular per-site Kd.
  • DnaA binding does NOT modulate intrinsic hydrolysis from Study 2. RIDA modulation arrives in Study 5.
  • Low-affinity boxes ONLY bind DnaA-ATP. The plan PDF is explicit on this.
  • Initial expectation that oriC binding starts when unoccupied chromosome sites drop to ~10 (within order of magnitude of the 11 oriC boxes).
